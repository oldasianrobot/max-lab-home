B — Camera Travels Through Cloud

Scroll position moves the camera through the 3D particle sphere. Hero = you're inside the dense core. As you scroll down, the camera pulls back or through — cloud density and color shifts by section. Feels like a journey.

Particles are distributed across the full page height. Different color zones align with different sections — cyan near Hero, periwinkle by Experiments, amber near Writing, violet near Contact. Scrolling reveals new cloud regions.
